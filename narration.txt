This is Shotlist, an open-source rebuild of Higgsfield's Cinema Studio, built on the Comfy Developer Platform with LTX 2.5 as the only video model.

Two modes. Text to video: describe a scene, and shot one is an LTX text-to-video render, sent exactly as you typed it. Its last frame becomes the source still, and every following shot animates from it, so the scene stays consistent.

Image to video: drop in a picture, pick director moves, crash zoom, dolly zoom, crane reveal, whip pan, and press Generate. Each shot is its own LTX render on Comfy Cloud, stitched into one trailer.

Change one move, and only that shot re-renders.

Every shot is a ComfyUI workflow in the repo. This narration was generated with the ElevenLabs API. MIT licensed. Clone it and make your own.
